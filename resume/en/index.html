Researcher & Builder Profile

Fu-Yuan Cheng

(@gloomcheng)

Founder & Software Architect　|　Ph.D. Candidate in Engineering Technology, NYUST

Fu-Yuan Cheng is a software architect, entrepreneur, and educator with over 15 years of experience spanning open-source systems, educational technology, and healthcare informatics. With an interdisciplinary background in Information Management and Design, he has founded FANSEE Studio (interactive AR educational storytelling) and WeeMed AI (clinical workflow automation with SMART on FHIR). A long-time open-source contributor and conference speaker, he specializes in problem definition, standards-based system architecture, and deploying resilient software to complex frontline environments.
