Researcher & Builder Profile

Fu-Yuan Cheng

(@gloomcheng)

Founder & Software Architect　|　Ph.D. Candidate in Engineering Technology, NYUST

Fu-Yuan Cheng is a software architect, entrepreneur, and educator with an interdisciplinary background in Information Management and Design. He is the founder of WeeMed AI (healthcare workflow automation and SMART on FHIR), FANSEE Studio (interactive AR educational storytelling, Golden Pin Design Award winner), and zhelang Co., Ltd. (named after the Taiwanese phrase for 'integrity/being a good human'). He has taught as an adjunct lecturer across multiple universities including NYUST, Providence University, and NFU, and has been a long-time open-source contributor and conference speaker.
