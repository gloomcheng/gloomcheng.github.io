Researcher & Builder Profile

Fu-Yuan Cheng

(@gloomcheng)

Founder & CEO, WeeMed AI · Founder & CEO, FANSEE Co., Ltd.　|　Ph.D. Candidate, NYUST

Fu-Yuan Cheng (gloomcheng) is an entrepreneur, software architect, and educator. He is currently a Ph.D. candidate at the Graduate School of Engineering Science and Technology, National Yunlin University of Science and Technology (NYUST). He serves as Founder & CEO of WeeMed AI, Founder & CEO of FANSEE Co., Ltd., and Founder & CTO of ZhèLâng Co., Ltd. His work focuses on healthcare information standards (SMART on FHIR), augmented reality (AR) interactive education, and the open-source community.
